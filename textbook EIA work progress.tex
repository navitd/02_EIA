\section{Uploading data from OECD and Statcan and Integrating Them}
\subsection{File $GDP_comp.py$}
In $GDP_comp.py$ I upload OECD data and Statcan data and integrate them by
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Converting Statcan from CAD to USD
    \item Mapping the Statcan sectors (more detailed) to OECD sectors
\end{enumerate}
